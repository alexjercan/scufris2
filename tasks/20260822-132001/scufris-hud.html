01

Design language

tokens // grammar

One rule set, borrowed from the best-documented systems in the research: input states react to you, processing states animate themselves, red belongs only to error and mic-off. Motion pattern - not hue - is what separates states, so everything survives grayscale. Sounds only at boundaries the eye can miss.

bg #181818ground
bg+1 #282828surfaces, bars
fg #e4e4eftext
yellow #ffdd33listening, identity
brown #cc8c3ctranscribing, labels
quartz #95a99freview, muted
niagara #96a6c8working
green #73c936speaking, ok
wisteria #9e95c7attention
red #f43841error, mic - reserved

Deviation from Alexa

Alexa says blue = listening, yellow = notification. Here yellow = listening, because gruber yellow is the identity and mic-open is the identity moment. Attention takes wisteria instead. Motion still disambiguates: listening reacts to your voice, attention pulses on its own.

Three layers, no WebGL

Orb core: CSS conic gradients, compositor-driven. Reactive layer: Canvas 2D waveform, running only while listening or speaking, sprung toward the 60ms Rust level tick - while you speak, the wave is the only truth, since no words exist yet. Transcript layer: partials stream the moment transcription starts. WebGL is banned: WebKitGTK silently software-renders.

Sound budget

Four earcons, ever: mic open (rising), mic close (falling), attention (one chime), error (one low tone). Nothing for working or speaking. A sound heard 50 times a day must be near-subliminal or it gets muted - Alexa shipped Brief Mode to fix exactly this. Review verdict: cues ship enabled from the start, with a mute switch.

03

On i3

shipping scope // v1 polish

Super+D while you work. The pill takes no focus - your editor keeps the cursor. An i3 binding mode captures esc and enter; everything else passes through. The bar shows the mode, and the journal tells the whole story.

04

Session HUD

v2 // the primary session surface

Scufris's own surface for the conversation - spawned by super+s or a click on the pill, itself a widget, mirroring the one authoritative Pi session over the control socket. Terminal discipline, no bubbles, no webview chat app. kitty + pi stays underneath as the debug view: the raw live terminal when you need to see exactly what the agent did. The popup remains authoritative until the mirror earns trust.

One brain, two surfaces - the state machine

Super+D and Super+S are not two modes of the same thing - they are two depths of one session. The pill is for anything that fits in one line and must not cost focus; the popup is for anything you read, copy, or continue. The yellow edge is the rule that justifies keeping both.

  • Pill earns its life with latency and focus preservation: fire-and-forget verbs, one-line answers, zero window churn.
  • Popup earns its life with depth: scrollback, copyable output, multi-turn. In v1 it is the session's own Kitty TUI; the target is the HUD widget as the primary surface (super+s or a pill click spawns it), with kitty + pi demoted to the debug view. The split stays either way.
  • The escalation ladder: a line in the pill, then exhibit widgets beside it, then the session surface raised - one spawn interface, increasing prominence. Whether an exhibit can be pinned into an instrument is an open question below.
  • The invariant: the pill is ephemeral and never holds state you would miss. Anything durable lives in the session, visible from any surface.
05

Exhibits + instruments

v3 concept // dashboardd embed

Not one overlay - two kinds of floating windows out of the same dashboardd runtime. Exhibits: Scufris spawns them himself as visual aids while he is talking - "cpu is at ninety percent" and the graph appears beside the pill, then fades with the topic. Instruments: you summon them - calendar, tasks - interactive, focused, alive until you close them. The agent feeds both, so nothing carries per-widget config.

Exhibit lifecycle - nothing vanishes mid-thought

An exhibit ages on topic relevance, not on the pill - closing the pill changes nothing on screen, because the pill is only the mic surface. When the subject moves on the exhibit dims instead of dying, lingers through a ~60s grace window, and only then goes - the exit itself can be a quick blink, because dimming already was the feedback. The invariant is not slow exits; it is that nothing drops straight out of LIVE. Speech freezes every clock: while the mic is hot or Scufris is talking, nothing dims and nothing fades, so you can read the numbers off an exhibit while you dictate. The only instant exits are explicit - the close tick on the exhibit, or a "clear" verb. Pinning settles the last open question: the pin tick promotes an exhibit into an instrument, which stops aging and is yours until closed.

06

Why these choices

research digest // 4 sweeps

Five durability laws

  1. Reactive beats proactive. Everything loved waits for the user; everything hated interrupts or captures on its own.
  2. The hotkey is the product. Instant summon is the one universally praised desktop-AI affordance.
  3. Local tools are the differentiator. One audited agent session beats a chat wrapper every time.
  4. Speed decides voice adoption. Sub-1.5s round trips or it dies by week two. Latency is the first animation.
  5. Ambient surfaces survive as live state, not decoration. Summoned overlays are kept; wallpaper dashboards rot.

Wake word

openWakeWord as a systemd user service (already in nixpkgs), poking the same start action Super+D uses. PipeWire shares the mic natively. Off by default, bar indicator while armed. Porcupine is dead to us (key-gated, free tier gone); build this only after the hotkey path saturates.

Focus-free keys

i3 binding mode over XGrabKey: no focus steal, no stuck grabs, free bar indicator, identical config on sway. The app exits the mode itself whenever it closes the pill. Fallback for other WMs: tauri global-shortcut, registered only while visible.

Logs + dev CLI

tracing + tracing-journald, auto-detected; --foreground forces pretty ANSI logs from the same binary. INFO = state transitions only, so journalctl --user -u scufris-desktop stays quiet and structured. Webview console forwarded at DEBUG.

Not building

  • a chat webview (the Electron backlash is unanimous)
  • launcher, clipboard, window management - rofi and i3 own them
  • avatar bodies - the one-hour ceiling
  • continuous screen recording - Rewind's own pivot is the verdict
  • sarcastic confirmations, assistant-initiated speech
  • wake word before push-to-talk saturates

Build order

1logging› 2pill visual› 3i3 mode› 4dictation + verbs + capture› 5wake word, HUD

Logging first because it unblocks live playtesting of everything after it. The pill second because it is the stated pain and every later feature reuses its state grammar.

Verdicts + what is still open

  1. Yellow = listening: confirmed. Review verdict 2026-08-25 - the Alexa convention carries no weight here; gruber yellow keeps the identity on the mic moment. Attention stays wisteria.
  2. Earcons: on from the start. Review verdict 2026-08-25 - the four boundary cues ship enabled (mic open, mic close, attention, error), near-subliminal, with a mute switch.
  3. Bare esc + enter: confirmed. Review verdict 2026-08-25 - while the pill is open nothing else is being touched, so bare keys it is. $mod variants stay the documented fallback if it ever bites.
  4. Session HUD (04): promoted. Second-pass verdict 2026-08-25 - the HUD widget becomes the primary way to read the session (super+s or a pill click spawns it), and kitty + pi demotes to the debug view. Transition gradually: the Kitty popup stays authoritative until the mirror earns trust.
  5. Pill personality: the dynamic glow. Review verdict 2026-08-25 - the state-colored glow is the personality. It rides the mic level while listening and breathes slowly at idle (the demo pill above does this now). No blink or oneko flourishes; reduced-motion disables the breathing.
  6. Exhibits: ephemeral with dimming as the feedback, pinnable by promotion. Review verdict 2026-08-25 - lifecycle drawn in 05: exhibits age on topic relevance, not on the pill; dim on topic change, ~60s grace, then a quick exit is fine (dimmed was the warning - the rule is no drop straight out of LIVE); every clock freezes while anyone is talking; close tick / "clear" verb are the only instant exits; the pin tick promotes an exhibit into an instrument.
