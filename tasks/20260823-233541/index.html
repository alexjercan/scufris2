3. What we learned

Two synthesis documents, backed by eight raw evidence reports written by parallel research agents. Read the syntheses; drop into a raw report when you want sources and details.

RESEARCH.mdcomponent judgments
Which existing tools to reuse, and why.
The capture shortlist (monolith, single-file-cli, yt-dlp, gallery-dl, trafilatura - all in nixpkgs), the dual-artifact rule (snapshot for display, extract for retrieval), the video pipeline your existing whisper-server already enables, the staged retrieval ladder (rg, then SQLite FTS5, embeddings only on evidence), reference display split by content type, and the parked khal+vdirsyncer calendar verdict. No new daemons anywhere.
MARKET.mdmarket lessons
What similar projects teach - especially the dead ones.
Nobody ships your combination; the den is the moat. Six reusable-now patterns (ArchiveBox's manifest layout, the MCP filesystem contract shape, Karakeep's CLI triad, the Wyoming protocol lesson) and eight ranked mistakes to avoid, led by Omnivore's 15-day death: does it still work after the acquihire?
research/capture.md
Web capture and archival tools, per-tool nixpkgs checks, format comparison, and why ArchiveBox-style all-in-one tools are wrong-shaped here.
research/ingestion.md
Multimodal ingestion: word-timestamp transcription, ffmpeg keyframes, docling for PDFs, GPU cost estimates, and the recommended local-only v1 pipeline.
research/retrieval.md
The retrieval ladder in depth: why rg is a legitimate v1, FTS5 as the embedded next step, and the concrete evidence gate before sqlite-vec embeddings.
research/reference-display.md
Grounded in dashboardd source: why iframes are structurally blocked, the sanitized tatr-tasks viewer precedent, and the browser-with-i3-rules path for live pages.
research/calendar.md
The parked calendar domain: khal + vdirsyncer as the presumptive answer, one .ics per event, agenda via khal list --json.
research/market-github.md
Sixteen open-source projects with live activity data: Leon, Khoj, OVOS, Open Interpreter, Reor, ArchiveBox, Omnivore, Karakeep and more - what to reuse, how they died.
research/market-web.md
Beyond GitHub: product postmortems (Rewind, Humane, Rabbit, Mycroft, Omnivore, Evernote), local-first essays, personal-RAG practitioner consensus, calm-technology research, explorable-explanations prior art.
research/ideas-raw.md
The raw 53-idea brainstorm across ten lenses, generated under the hard constraints. Curated into IDEAS.md below.
